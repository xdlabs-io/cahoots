Clear space and size

Keep the height of the letter "o" free on every side. Minimum wordmark width: 90px on screen, 25mm in print. Mascot icon: 24px. App icon: 16px (use favicon.ico).

Do
  • Use lowercase "cahoots" everywhere in prose.
  • Use the sun shadow on light backgrounds.
  • Use a mono file for one-color print.
Don't
  • Write "Cahoots" or "CAHOOTS" in the wordmark.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.
  • Set the sun-colored wordmark on paper (1.42:1).
  • Swap the font or redraw the mascot.

4. Voice

Playful on the surface, strict underneath: board game night with a notary. Plain words, short sentences, at most one joke. Say what was recorded, by whom and when. Never hide dissent.

Sounds like
  • agent 2 objects. noted.
  • you're in cahoots.
  • dissent is welcome here.
Avoid
  • "revolutionary", "seamless", "supercharge"
  • exclamation chains, emoji walls

5. Motion

cahoots

Letters pop in left to right with a 140ms stagger and a small overshoot, then the tagline fades in and the stamp lands. Loop 5.5s on marketing pages; play once in product. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.
